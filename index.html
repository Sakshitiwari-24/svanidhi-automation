Automation Highlights Zero-Error Safety

  • Extra Portal Scheme Tolerance (Fixed): Automatically ignores schemes on the portal that are already Sanctioned or not present in the current Excel batch (`--allow-extra-portal` enabled by default).
  • Two-Phase Verification: Enters Reference No in the Apply dialog, verifies stage transitions to Applied, then confirms Sanction and verifies Sanctioned before updating Excel.
  • Scheme Name Alias Matching: Normalizes scheme variations (`PM Suraksha Bima For Mother/Father`) automatically across Excel and Portal.
  • Automatic Excel Backups & Resume: Creates timestamped `.backup-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.xlsx` copies before every run and safely skips completed rows.
